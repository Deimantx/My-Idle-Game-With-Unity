# Vision Statement

Create a modern, UI-focused idle RPG that delivers the long-term progression and satisfaction of classic MMORPGs while remaining approachable as a idle game.

Players should always feel like they are making meaningful progress, whether they are actively playing or offline. Every action should contribute toward unlocking new skills, equipment, areas, companions, and long-term account progression.

The game should be easy to understand in the first five minutes but deep enough to keep players engaged for hundreds or even thousands of hours.

---

# Design Pillars

Every feature added to the game should support these pillars.

## 1. Long-Term Progression

The player should never truly "finish" the game.

There should always be:

- another level
- another item
- another achievement
- another area
- another boss
- another skill
- another collection to complete

Progression is the heart of the game.

---

## 2. Meaningful Choices

The player should frequently make decisions.

Examples:

- Which skill should I train?
- Should I craft or sell resources?
- Should I fight monsters or improve professions?
- Which companion should I bring?
- Which equipment should I create first?

Avoid gameplay where there is only one obvious best choice.

---

## 3. Connected Skills

Most Skills should interact with one another.

Examples:

Woodcutting → Carpentry

Mining → Smithing

Fishing → Cooking

Hunting → Leatherworking

Farming + Foraging → Herblore

Enchanting → All Equipment

---

## 4. Idle First

The game is designed around idle gameplay.

The player chooses activities.

The game performs them automatically.

The player returns to collect rewards and make new decisions.

Active play should improve efficiency, used in most hardest combat encounters mainly for combat, but idle play should always feel rewarding.

---

## 5. Easy to Learn, Difficult to Master

The first hour should be simple.

As the player progresses they unlock:

- more mechanics
- more optimization
- better equipment
- mastery systems
- companions
- endgame progression

Complexity should grow naturally.

---

## 6. Respect Player Time

The game should never punish players for taking breaks.

Offline progress should always be valuable.

---

# Player Fantasy

The player is building an adventurer from nothing into a legendary master of every profession.

Rather than controlling a character directly, the player acts as a strategist, deciding what the character should learn, craft, gather, fight, and discover.

Over time the player develops a complete account capable of mastering every profession and conquering every challenge.

# Long-Term Goals

Players should work toward goals that may take weeks or months.

Examples:

Max every skill.

Unlock every companion.

Collect every item.

Complete every achievement.

Defeat every boss.

Finish every collection log.

Craft legendary equipment.

Reach mastery in every profession.

---

# What Makes This Game Different

Although inspired by idle RPGs, the goal is not to recreate another game.

This project focuses on:

A deeper connection between professions.

Companions that influence every activity.

More satisfying progression loops.

Modern UI.

Greater interaction between combat and non-combat skills.

A stronger feeling of building one persistent character over months or years.

---

# Things We Will NOT Do

Avoid:

Meaningless grinding.

Complicated systems without purpose.

Dozens of duplicate resources.

Artificial waiting with no player decisions.

---

# Development Philosophy

Build systems before content.

Make everything data-driven.

Reuse systems whenever possible.

Every new feature should integrate with existing systems instead of creating isolated mechanics.

Focus on quality rather than quantity.

Prototype first.

Polish later.

---

# Success Criteria

The project is successful if:

Players always have meaningful goals.

Progress feels rewarding.

Skills feel interconnected.

The UI is clean and intuitive.

Offline progress feels satisfying.

Every session feels productive.

The game remains expandable for years without major redesigns.
